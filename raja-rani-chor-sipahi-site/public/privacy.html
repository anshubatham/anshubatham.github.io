Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Raja Rani Chor Sipahi mobile application (the “App”), developed and published by EassyCode (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our App. This document outlines our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our App is designed to offer a traditional paper-and-pencil game experience digitally. We do not require users to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (such as real name, home address, or email address) to play the game.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics, game connectivity, and advertising purposes.

2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services to support gameplay, multiplayer features, and monetization. These services have their own Privacy Policies governing data collection:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to serve banner, interstitial, app open, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse location, and app usage data to personalize and optimize ads. Google Privacy Policy | AdMob data collection information
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google): Used to handle live game state, room creation, scores, and real-time multiplayer features. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services: Used for achievements, leaderboards, and game progress authorization. Google Play Services Privacy Policy

3. Types of Data Collected

  • Device & Usage Data: Operating system version, device model, unique device identifiers (for example, Advertising ID), and app interaction data.
  • Game Data: In-game scores, player nicknames chosen during rooms, and game-state metrics stored temporarily for multiplayer sessions.
  • Log & Diagnostic Data: In the event of an app error or crash, diagnostic data may be automatically generated for performance improvements.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us, and we will promptly delete such information from our records.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Temporary game-session data stored in Firebase is automatically purged or overwritten periodically. Since we do not collect personal user accounts or store user identities, non-identifiable usage data is retained only as long as necessary by third-party services (Google AdMob / Firebase) to deliver their services.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us with device access for gameplay. We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting data, but no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.

8. Contact Us

For questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact EassyCode at batham925@gmail.com.
