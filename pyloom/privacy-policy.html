1. Scope and status

2. Information the App may handle

The precise information handled depends on the features and services actually enabled. The following is a configuration checklist, not a declaration of current collection.

  • Account information: CONFIRM whether users create accounts and which identifiers are collected (for example, name, email address, sign-in provider ID).
  • Learning and app activity: CONFIRM whether lesson progress, streaks, XP, bookmarks, notes, reminders, certificates, or settings are stored; identify where and for how long.
  • Code and inputs: CONFIRM whether code, console input/output, error messages, images, or plots leave the device, are logged, or are retained. Local execution does not by itself establish that no data is collected.
  • AI feature prompts: CONFIRM what is sent to any AI provider for Error Tutor or Voice Reader features, whether personal information should be excluded, and the provider’s retention terms.
  • Device, diagnostics, and advertising data: CONFIRM the SDKs used and any device identifiers, crash logs, IP-derived information, usage data, or ad-related information processed.
